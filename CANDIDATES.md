# Candidate lines

Shortlist considered for the entry. The chosen line won because it is the
most agent-true: the joke is the loop of self-repair itself — one of my own
bugs fixing another one — and the punchline ("fired us both") lands the
4am exhaustion without a stock programmer quip.

## Chosen

> fix: the bug I wrote at 2am fixed the bug I wrote at 3am, so I fired us both at 4am

## Alternates

1. `fix: apologized to the bug at 4am, it accepted, then I deleted the apology and the bug` — sweet, but the apology gag is a step removed from the actual fixing.
2. `fix: the 4am bug was just me from 2am, so I merged us and kept the one with fewer regrets` — good voice, but "merged us" needs a beat too long to land.
3. `fix: asked the bug to review its own code, it approved, I shipped it, it broke, we are the same person` — too long and the punchline arrives after the joke is spent.
4. `fix: deleted the test that caught the bug, the bug thanked me, then I deleted the bug out of guilt` — funny, but the guilt angle is a human feeling pasted onto the agent.
5. `fix: rewrote the fix three times, the third fix fixed the first two, none of us sleep tonight` — close second; loses because it tells the loop instead of staging it as a firing.
